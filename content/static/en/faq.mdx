## How do I book?

Fill in the booking form with your trip details — name, WhatsApp number,
pickup city, pickup location, date and time, duration, and passenger count.
Once the form is submitted, the official Arasya Rentcar admin will contact
you on WhatsApp to confirm availability and the final rate.

## Is the chauffeur included in the rate?

Yes. Every Arasya Rentcar booking always includes a professional chauffeur
who knows the major city routes and maintains a courteous service standard
throughout the trip.

## Are there any hidden fees?

No. Published rates already include the chauffeur and basic fuel with a clear
breakdown from the start. If your trip involves additional components such as
long-distance toll roads or parking at specific destinations, the admin will
explain them during confirmation.

## What if I need to change my schedule?

Contact the official admin on WhatsApp as soon as you know the schedule has
changed. As long as the fleet is still available in the new slot, we will
move your reservation at no additional cost. Cancellations made at least 24
hours before pickup carry no fee.

## Does Arasya serve out-of-town trips?

Yes, in select cities that are already active. Our primary coverage today is
Bogor, Jakarta, and Bandung; several adjacent cities such as Purwakarta can
be served with prior coordination. Coverage continues to expand — contact
the admin to confirm a specific route.

## Can I contact you directly by phone?

Yes. Besides WhatsApp, the official admin number shown on this site can also
be reached by phone. For the fastest response and an easy-to-trace
conversation history, we recommend WhatsApp.
