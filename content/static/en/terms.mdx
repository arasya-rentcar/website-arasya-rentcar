## Service scope

Arasya Rentcar provides chauffeur car rental with a professional driver. Every
reservation processed through this site always includes an experienced
chauffeur and a regularly maintained vehicle.

## Booking process

1. You fill in the booking form with your trip details.
2. The system forwards the request to the official admin on WhatsApp.
3. The admin confirms availability, the final rate, and pickup details.
4. The reservation is active only after written confirmation from the
   official admin.

## Rates and payment

Published rates already include the chauffeur and basic fuel with a clear
breakdown. Additional components (long-distance toll roads, parking at
specific destinations, driver lodging for multi-day trips) will be explained
by the admin during confirmation. Payment must only be made to the account
named by the official admin through the official WhatsApp channel.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

Cancellations or schedule changes submitted at least 24 hours before the
pickup time carry no fee. Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance
may incur an administrative fee per the admin's confirmation.

## Liability

Arasya Rentcar operations follow Indonesia's prevailing transportation
regulations. The chauffeur observes traffic rules and maintains courteous
passenger service. Damage caused by passenger negligence and not covered by
the standard insurance will be handled separately during confirmation.

## Dispute resolution

Disputes are resolved first through good-faith discussion between the
customer and the official admin. If no agreement is reached, disputes are
resolved under the law applicable in Indonesia.
