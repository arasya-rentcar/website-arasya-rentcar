## Data we collect

Arasya Rentcar collects the minimum data needed to process a booking request:

- Full name and WhatsApp number from the booking form
- Trip details (pickup city and location, date, time, duration, passenger
  count)
- Optional notes you type into the form yourself
- An anonymised hash of your IP address for rate-limit enforcement

We do not collect government identity data, travel documents, or payment
information through this site.

## Why we process this data

- To process and confirm your booking via the official admin WhatsApp
- To prevent form abuse using a temporarily stored hashed IP
- To measure aggregate page interest through privacy-friendly analytics (no
  tracking cookies), only if you explicitly grant consent

## Data retention

- Booking data is retained for at most 180 days, then automatically deleted
  or anonymised
- Rate-limit IP hashes are retained for at most 24 hours
- Your analytics consent decision is stored in your device's localStorage;
  you can clear it at any time through your browser settings

## Third-party recipients

- **Supabase** as our managed database provider for booking storage and
  rate-limit hashes
- **Plausible** as our page analytics provider, used only when you grant
  consent
- **WhatsApp** when you click a `wa.me` link on this site, which starts the
  chat session directly in the WhatsApp application

We do not sell or rent your data to any third party.

## Access and deletion requests

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of any booking
data we still hold. Send such requests to the official admin WhatsApp number
shown on this site, including the name and WhatsApp number used at booking
time. We will process the request within seven business days.

## Cookies

This site does not use third-party tracking cookies. The analytics consent
banner stores your decision in your device's localStorage for one year.
