Toyota Innova is an Arasya favourite for families and small teams who need generous cabin space without trading away ride comfort. Seven roomy seats, dual-blower air conditioning, and a rear boot sized for four large suitcases suit it to the Jakarta to Bogor and Puncak corridor and the Jakarta to Bandung run via the Cipularang toll on weekdays.

<Callout>Every Arasya Innova is chauffeur-only. We do not release keys to guests because our focus is a safe, guided journey with a trained driver for the entire booking window.</Callout>

The fleet is refreshed on a thirty-six month rotation and no unit in rotation is older than four years of service age. Our partner workshop checks brakes, tyre pressure, headlights, and AC charge before each departure. Three-point seatbelts on every row, dual front airbags, ABS, and a reversing camera come standard on every unit. Cabins are disinfected after each trip and interior scent is kept deliberately neutral, so children and guests with mild allergies stay comfortable across longer routes.

Our drivers are trained for cross-city work. They know the reliable Jagorawi rest stops, the Puncak contraflow schedule on weekend afternoons, and the best Cikampek detours during long holiday weekends. For Soekarno-Hatta pickups, the chauffeur tracks your arrival through the official airport app and waits at the Terminal 3 meeting point, at no extra parking cost to you.

<Tip>Let us know your child-seat requirements at least twenty-four hours before departure so we can fit an ECE R44 compliant seat on the day at no additional charge.</Tip>

Many guests choose the Innova for weekend family escapes, small-team client visits, or same-day business trips to Bandung with one consistent driver from start to finish.
