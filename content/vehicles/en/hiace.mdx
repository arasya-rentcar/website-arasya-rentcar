Toyota Hiace Commuter is the pick when the group gets large, from wedding families coordinating guest shuttles to companies running weekend team retreats outside the city. Fourteen properly spaced seats, a high-capacity central air-conditioning unit, and a rear boot sized for ten large suitcases carry the whole team without splitting into a multi-car convoy that would otherwise get separated on the highway.

<Callout>Every Arasya Hiace is chauffeur-only. A uniformed driver with cross-city route experience accompanies every trip, so we do not offer a keys-only option on any unit in this van class.</Callout>

The fleet is rotated on a regular schedule and no Hiace in service is older than four years of operating life. Before each trip a partner technician checks brakes, tyre pressure, headlights, every row of seatbelts, and central AC circulation. Each unit ships with driver airbag, ABS, three-point belts on every row, laminated glass, and an emergency hammer within reach of each sliding door. Interiors are steam-cleaned after each outing so passengers with sensitive airways travel comfortably across longer intercity routes.

Our Hiace drivers are trained for convoy work across many scenarios: radio coordination between cars, tight car-park manoeuvres at busy wedding venues, and the patience to wait for guests leaving extended photo sessions on location. They also know the trusted rest stops along the Cipularang toll and the Trans-Java corridor, so prayer, meal, and stretch breaks stay on schedule throughout the itinerary.

<Tip>For large groups, share the guest manifest and multi-point pickup list at least forty-eight hours before departure so our driver can plan the most efficient combined route for the day.</Tip>

Hiace often serves Bandung to Garut group tours, Bogor wedding guest shuttles, and cross-city corporate roadshows along the Trans-Java corridor with the same driver throughout the event arc.
