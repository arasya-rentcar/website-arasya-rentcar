Airport transfers are where first impressions form. Guests arriving after long-haul flights need the certainty of a clean vehicle, a polite chauffeur, and a definite route to their destination. Arasya's **chauffeur-only airport transfer** covers Soekarno-Hatta (CGK) and Halim Perdanakusuma with live flight tracking that stays active from booking confirmation until the traveler reaches the final address across Jakarta or nearby cities.

The moment a booking code is issued, our system binds the guest's flight number to the driver's route plan. If the aircraft is delayed or lands early, the pickup slides automatically without the traveler needing to resend a heads-up, because our operations desk is already receiving the same real-time notification from the airline feed.

<Callout variant="success" title="Grace window">We grant a 30-minute waiting window after wheels down, enough time for immigration, baggage claim, and optional quarantine checks, with no extra fee as long as it falls inside the contract terms.</Callout>

Drivers wait in the official holding area with a name board listing the guest's initials whenever the greeter add-on is selected. Vehicle choice reflects the group size: an Innova Reborn for small families, a Hiace Premio for corporate teams or luggage-heavy groups, and a Camry sedan for executives seeking an extra layer of comfort. Every car clears a morning inspection covering cabin cleaning, tire pressure, and bottled water refills before its first trip.

Every toll and parking receipt is scanned during the trip and bundled into the final invoice. Travelers don't need to carry cash because driver tips can flow through the same channel when selected. This transparency is why travel agents and executive assistants pick Arasya as the long-term transfer partner for business and family clients who cycle through Jakarta and the companion cities close by.
