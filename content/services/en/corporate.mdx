Modern enterprises can't afford to stitch together ad hoc rides. Sales leads chasing pitch meetings along Sudirman, directors running back-to-back reviews across SCBD, and HR teams collecting candidates at the airport all need a predictable rhythm. Arasya's **corporate contract service** delivers that rhythm through dedicated chauffeurs, freshly detailed sedans or MPVs, and a billing flow that fits comfortably into any finance team's monthly close.

Every contract begins with a short intake call between our account lead and your PIC. We map the daily route, standby location at the office lobby, and the service tone your chauffeur should project, from formal greetings in English to specific dress codes that reinforce your company branding. The intake becomes a written SLA that obliges our operations team to arrive on time at the office lobby or executive's residence each morning.

<Callout variant="info" title="Automatic backup">Monthly contracts include standby cover whenever the primary driver takes leave or falls ill, so your executive calendar remains intact without last-minute scrambling from your assistant.</Callout>

Corporate service lives in the details. Vehicles are detailed every morning before the shift begins, luggage sits on scratch-free liners in the trunk, and drivers memorise alternative routes whenever rallies or major events close downtown streets. For intercity agendas such as Jakarta to Bandung or Bogor to Puncak, we pre-plan chauffeur rest windows so driver hours stay compliant with Manpower Ministry regulations and passenger safety stays the top priority.

<Tip>Ask our sales desk to bundle an airport standby package for visiting executives so delegations don't need a separate ride the moment they land at CGK.</Tip>

Monthly reports arrive as Excel or PDF documents, ready for internal audit workflows. Each trip is logged with pickup location, destination, start and end times, distance covered, and toll or parking costs supported by original receipts. Our account manager stays available for any reconciliation questions your finance team raises before invoice approval, keeping the partnership free of administrative friction.
